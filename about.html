About Us

Registered exporter of genuine industrial electrical components

Who We Are

Tuochen Industrial (抚州市临川区拓辰贸易商行 / Fuzhou Linchuan Tuochen Trading Firm) is an officially registered exporter based in Fuzhou and Guangzhou, China, specializing in brand new, genuine industrial electrical components.

We supply the parts a control panel needs — miniature circuit breakers, residual current breakers (RCBO), AC contactors, thermal relays, intermediate relays, timer switches, photoelectric sensors and DIN-rail terminal blocks from CHINT (正泰), DELIXI (德力西) and OMRON.

Why Buyers Trust Us

  • Registered exporter: We are a licensed business entity in China with complete export credentials — not an anonymous seller.
  • Genuine parts: Products are sourced from authorized industrial distributors, brand new and authentic. No clones, no counterfeits.
  • Accurate specs: Every listing shows the correct model number, ratings and direct-replacement compatibility, so you order the right part the first time.
  • Small batches welcome: Sample and small-batch orders are accepted — test our quality before committing to larger quantities.
  • Export ready: Commercial invoice, packing list and brand authenticity documents with every order.

How We Work

  1. Inquiry — send us your brand + model number on WhatsApp or email.
  2. Confirmation — we confirm stock, price, specs and photos.
  3. Payment — WorldFirst B2B T/T to our Hong Kong JPMorgan account (fast, low-cost) or China bank account.
  4. Shipment — packed securely, shipped by international courier or air/sea for bulk.
