About Us

Your trusted source for tested used Japanese servo systems

Who We Are

Tuochen Trade (抚州市临川区拓辰贸易商行) is a trading company based in Fuzhou and Guangzhou, China, specializing in used and refurbished Japanese servo motors and servo drives.

We focus on the three brands most in demand across Asian factories — Mitsubishi MR-J, Panasonic MINAS and Yaskawa Sigma. Units are sourced from maintained production lines, factory pull-outs and verified recyclers in the Guangzhou–Shenzhen industrial belt.

What We Guarantee

  • Tested, not guessed: Every unit is bench-tested — insulation resistance, encoder signal and load run. You receive a test video of your exact unit.
  • Real photos: No stock photos. We show you the actual item with its serial number before you pay.
  • Inspection period: 30-day inspection period. Defective units replaced or refunded.
  • Honest grading: A-grade (tested, minor cosmetics) / B-grade (tested, scratches or stains) — priced accordingly.
  • Export ready: Commercial invoice, packing list, test report and photos with every order. FOB Guangzhou.

How We Work

  1. Inquiry — send us brand + model number on WhatsApp.
  2. Confirmation — we confirm stock, condition, price and send photos/video.
  3. Payment — 30% deposit (T/T or WorldFirst), balance before shipment.
  4. Shipment — packed with care, shipped by sea or air to your port.
